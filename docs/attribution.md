# Attribution

HolyCow combines Matt Pocock's discovery and delivery workflows with selected engineering guidance from Lauren Tan's pstack and Addy Osmani's agent-skills. The [design review](skill-design-review.md) records the pinned sources, decisions for all ten skills, and boundaries retained for portability and context efficiency.

`holycow-guide` adapts the situation-based skill and workflow selection in Matt Pocock's [ask-matt](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/ask-matt/SKILL.md), including reading current skill definitions before recommending a route. HolyCow uses its own skill boundaries, keeps setup optional, and avoids prescribing context resets or a full sequence for small tasks.

`holycow-init` adapts the inspect-first repository onboarding, tracker configuration, canonical documentation pointers, and preservation of existing instructions in Matt Pocock's [setup-matt-pocock-skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/setup-matt-pocock-skills/SKILL.md). HolyCow adds a shared optional context consumed by its workflow skills, command and capability checks scoped to the current harness, and explicit limits on what setup verification establishes.

`hc-refine` adapts the decision dependencies, question rounds, and shared-understanding confirmation in Matt Pocock's [grilling skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md), used by [grill-me](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/productivity/grill-me/SKILL.md). The referenced revision is `5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2`.

The terminology checks and selective ADR guidance adapt his [domain-modeling skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/domain-modeling/SKILL.md), composed with grilling by [grill-with-docs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs/SKILL.md). HolyCow makes document updates optional and follows the project's existing conventions.

HolyCow adds explicit evidence status, partial-answer handling, bounded scope, and a prototype handoff without building the prototype. Research delegation follows the user's authorization.

`hc-compass` adapts the evolving decision map, dependencies, and focused resolution methods from Matt Pocock's [wayfinder skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/wayfinder/SKILL.md) at the same revision. HolyCow follows the user's tracker choice with a local fallback, discloses missing companion skills, and preserves the user's authorization for delegation and implementation.

`hc-lab` adapts the question-driven experiments, UI comparisons, and interactive state exploration in Matt Pocock's [prototype skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/prototype/SKILL.md), including its [UI](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/prototype/UI.md) and [logic](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/prototype/LOGIC.md) guidance at the same revision. HolyCow adds explicit evidence limits, diagram-based exploration, and verification appropriate to the question, while following the user's repository workflow.

`hc-plan` adapts the context synthesis and testing-boundary guidance in Matt Pocock's [to-spec](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/to-spec/SKILL.md), and the vertical slices, blocking dependencies, and compatibility migrations in [to-tickets](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/to-tickets/SKILL.md), at the same revision. HolyCow scales the brief to the work, preserves unresolved decisions, and reconciles existing records and partial tracker writes.

`hc-build` adapts the scoped implementation, checks, and review handoff in Matt Pocock's [implement](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/implement/SKILL.md), the behavior-focused red-green guidance in [tdd](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/tdd/SKILL.md), and the symptom-driven investigation, targeted probes, and regression verification in [diagnosing-bugs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/diagnosing-bugs/SKILL.md), at the same revision. HolyCow follows existing testing boundaries and repository authorization, reviews uncommitted work, and reports incomplete verification explicitly. Debugging loads conditionally, respects diagnosis-only requests, and avoids fixed hypothesis counts or unsupported certainty.

`hc-review` adapts the separate requirements and standards perspectives and independent reviewers in Matt Pocock's [code-review](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/code-review/SKILL.md) at the same revision. HolyCow adds explicit support for pending and staged content, correctness tracing, evidence-based prioritization, and direct review when delegation is unavailable or not authorized. Findings are reconciled and ordered by impact while retaining their perspectives.

`hc-pr` adapts the visual explanation, before/after evidence, and merge-risk guidance in Matt Pocock's [pr](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/pr/SKILL.md) at the same revision. His visual guidance credits Dex Horthy of HumanLayer and [show-me](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills/blob/653b6411c1f70c275a18e37673b042ff99f67ceb/plugins/show-me/skills/show-me/SKILL.md); that upstream credit is retained. HolyCow follows repository templates, records evidence limitations, and integrates with Build without duplicating its delivery workflow.

`hc-retro` adapts the session-evidence approach and environment-improvement categories in Matt Pocock's [retro](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2/skills/engineering/retro/SKILL.md) at the same revision. HolyCow adds explicit evidence limits, proportionate remedies, private versus reusable lessons, and a distinction between applied changes and demonstrated behavioral improvement.
