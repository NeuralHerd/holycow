# BossGoat

BossGoat brings your coding agents, tasks, and decisions into one view inside Herdr. Give each project an Assistant, describe the work, and follow what needs you, what is working, and what is ready.

## How it works

You set the direction. The Assistant turns it into tasks. Your chosen agents work with HolyCow guidance and bring back progress, questions, and evidence. Herdr runs their sessions; BossGoat coordinates the work; HolyCow supplies the method.

## What can it do?

- **Keep tasks visible.** See what is working, ready, or waiting for your answer.
- **Choose agents by skill.** Use Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor, with your own model choices for HolyCow skills.
- **Bring review and evidence together.** Enable review, inspect proof, and choose how work is accepted and merged.
- **Connect your projects and machines.** Keep an Assistant for each space, with agents running in herdr-core even when the app window closes. Use the optional web app for browser access.

## Get started

Follow the [Recommended Setup](herdr.md) to install Herdr, BossGoat, and HolyCow, then choose your agents and task settings.
